## Head and pipeline findings

- v1.1 and v2.1 are executable end-to-end ONNX classifiers. For both, the ONNX graph is the only locally verified complete DR classifier artifact; standalone raw head checkpoints/feature matrices for the historical app assessments were not recovered.
- v1.1 takes RGB NCHW float32 `[B,3,512,512]`, using its documented Pillow RGB decode, BILINEAR square resize, `/255`, ImageNet normalization, and planar channel order. It applies `>= 0.48` to the graph output. No additional calibration is recorded outside the model.
- v2.1 takes RGB NCHW float32 `[1,3,512,512]`. Its separate OpenCV path performs BGR→RGB, a 512 long-edge proxy, foreground crop when supported, centered square padding, 640 then 512 `INTER_AREA` resize, ImageNet normalization, and NCHW packing. The graph includes scaler, MLP, and Platt parameters; Android consumes the probability once and applies `>= 0.3675977844516245`.
- The v2.1 checkpoint provenance points to KD Run 2 final. The app MLP is not part of the KD encoder checkpoint; the checkpoint contains the student and projection. The v1.1 graph uses a logistic-regression classifier, but its original training feature ordering, fitting dataset, and separate checkpoint were not found.
- The previous full-run report in `kd_run2/full_out/out/` and release/checkpoint manifests establish that KD Run 2 did run. `kd_run2/WORKFILE.md` still says “Full run NOT launched”; that note is stale and conflicts with its later full-run result section and actual output summaries.

## Reuse recommendation

For an eventual DR head experiment, recover the hash-verified KD Run 2 checkpoint and first audit whether the cached KD768 features can be joined to an approved label and patient-grouped partition. Compare a frozen KD768 head-only baseline against the frozen v2.1 ONNX baseline on a separate development cohort. Preserve v1.1 and v2.1 as unchanged references. Do not use current cached-probe test rows or historical app assessments to tune a threshold or claim acceptance.
